## STEP 2: GPU (ADRENO 740) ADAPTATION: prepare now, test after Step 1
The msm driver already binds the display and prints `no GPU device was found`; the GPU is simply not in our DTS. Known from logs (FACT): `3da0000.iommu` (adreno_smmu) probe-defers (-517) on every boot, and sync_state is pending for `gpu_cc-sm8550` and `3d6a000.gmu`, i.e. the GPU nodes exist in `sm8550.dtsi` but are disabled. Plan (INFERENCE where marked; verify each against the real upstream files in `../_scratch` and the kernel tree):
1. Read how upstream SM8550 boards enable the GPU (`sm8550-qrd.dts` / `sm8550-hdk.dts`, `sm8550.dtsi`): enabling `&gpu` and `&gmu`, the `zap-shader` child with `memory-region = <&gpu_micro_code_mem>` and `firmware-name` (INFERENCE: `qcom/sm8550/a740_zap.mbn`), supplies and power domains (CX/GX via rpmhpd, GMU/RGMU), `gpucc`. Compare with our `dts/dm1q/dm1q.dts` reserved-memory map and with Samsung's downstream values in `docs/05-dm1q-real-hardware-values.md` and the crDroid DTS snapshot in `_scratch` (does the `gpu_micro_code_mem` carveout match this phone's layout? Samsung's secure `uh-heap`/`uh-guest` regions are a standing reset risk, see docs/06).
2. Firmware (INFERENCE on names, verify): the A740 uses the "gen70500" firmware family from linux-firmware (`qcom/gen70500_sqe.fw`, `qcom/gmu/gen70500_gmu.bin`, check the exact paths in the installed kernel's `drivers/gpu/drm/msm/adreno/a6xx_catalog.c`), plus the **zap shader**, which is vendor-signed: take it from this phone's own vendor partition (`a740_zap.*` under the Samsung vendor firmware dirs, split `.b00-.bNN` form needs joining with mdt tools) and check whether the signature is accepted on this locked-OEM-keys device. Zap loading needs QCOM_SCM/PAS and the MDT loader (verify they are enabled in `config-dm1q.fragment`).

Safest test path: a DT-only image set (`scripts/make-dtb-image-set.sh`, repo DTS stays canonical, experiment lives in `../_scratch`) with `&gpu`/`&gmu` enabled and firmware placed under `/lib/firmware/qcom/` on the phone (remember rule 3: touching `/lib/firmware` triggers the initramfs-extra regeneration), flash boot+vendor_boot from TWRP, read dmesg with `collect-phone-state.sh <label> --display`.
